## Overview

The **Conversations** page is your complete interaction history. Every time an AI Assistant has a conversation, whether a phone call or a website chat widget, it's logged here automatically.

## Conversation types

Orato logs two types of conversations:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="🎙️ Voice Conversations">
    Phone calls handled by your AI Assistant, both inbound calls to your Orato number and outbound calls made through Campaigns.

    Each voice conversation shows:

    - **Contact name** and source
    - **Duration** (e.g. 1:22, 0:38)
    - **Summary**: AI-generated summary of what was discussed
    - **Outcome**: result of the conversation (Qualified, Unknown, Not Interested, etc.)
    - **Date & Time**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="💬 Chat Conversations">
    Text-based interactions through your embedded Web Widget. Visitors who type instead of speaking are logged as Chat Conversations.

    Chat conversations have the same fields as voice:  contact, duration, summary, outcome, and timestamp.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The stats bar at the top shows a breakdown:

| Stat | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Conversations** | All voice \+ chat conversations combined |
| **Voice Conversations** | Phone call interactions only |
| **Chat Conversations** | Web widget text interactions only |

## Browsing conversations

<Frame>
  ![Browse Conver](/images/Browse-Conver.png)
</Frame>

The conversation table has these columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | Name of the person your assistant spoke with |
| **Type** | `Voice` (phone call) or `Chat` (web widget) |
| **Duration** | Length of the conversation in minutes:seconds |
| **Summary** | AI-generated summary of the conversation |
| **Outcome** | Result tagged at the end of the call |
| **Date & Time** | When the conversation happened |
| **Actions** | Click **View** to open the full transcript |

### Filtering conversations

Use the filters at the top to narrow down what you see:

- **Mode**: filter by Voice or Chat
- **Search**: search by visitor/contact name

## Viewing a conversation

Click **View** on any conversation to open its detail page. Inside you'll find:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Full transcript">
    A word-for-word log of the conversation with speaker labels, your AI Assistant's lines and the contact's responses, in order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recording (voice calls)">
    An audio recording of the phone call. Listen back for quality review, training, or compliance purposes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="AI summary">
    A concise AI-generated summary of what was discussed, useful for getting context quickly without reading the full transcript.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Outcome">
    The result the assistant determined: Qualified, Not Interested, Follow-up needed, etc. Outcomes are set based on your assistant's instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Metadata">
    Contact details, call timestamp, duration, and which AI Assistant handled the conversation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Conversation outcomes

Outcomes are automatically tagged by your AI Assistant based on how the conversation went:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualified** | Lead met your qualification criteria |
| **Not Interested** | Contact explicitly declined |
| **Unknown** | Outcome could not be determined |
| **Follow-up** | Needs another touchpoint |
| **Booked** | Meeting or appointment was scheduled |

<Tip>
  Define clear outcome conditions in your AI Assistant's instructions to improve outcome accuracy. For example: _"If the lead confirms a budget above \$5,000, mark them as Qualified."_
</Tip>
