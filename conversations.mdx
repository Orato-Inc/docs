**Conversations** is the log of every interaction your assistants have: phone calls, widget voice calls, and widget chats.

<Frame>
  ![Conversations page](/images/app/conversations.png)
</Frame>

## Summary cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total conversations** | All interactions |
| **Voice** | Voice conversations from the widget |
| **Chat** | Text conversations from the widget |

## The conversation list

Each row shows:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | Phone number or visitor |
| **Type** | Voice or chat |
| **Status** | For example **Completed** |
| **Assistant** | The assistant that handled it |
| **Duration** | Length of the conversation |
| **Summary** | AI-generated summary |
| **Outcome** | Result of the conversation |
| **Date & time** | When it happened |

Filter by status, mode (voice or chat), outcome, and date range, or search by contact. Click **Refresh** to load the latest conversations and **Export** to download them.

## Conversation details

Click **View** on any row to see the contact, type, assistant, time, and **Billing**: call cost, duration, and billable minutes.

<Note>
  Billable minutes are rounded up. For example, a 29-second call shows 1 billable minute.
</Note>

## Use conversations to improve your assistant

- Read conversations with a poor outcome and find where the assistant lost the caller.
- Look for questions it couldn't answer, and add them to the [knowledge base](/knowledge-base).
- Spot repeated objections and script responses in the [prompt](/prompting-guide).
