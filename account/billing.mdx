Orato uses a prepaid **wallet** in USD. Calls and phone numbers are paid from your wallet balance.

<Frame>
  ![Billing & Credits](/images/app/settings-billing.png)
</Frame>

## Check your balance

Your balance is always visible in the top bar of the app. Full details are in **Settings → Billing & Credits**, which shows the **Current balance** in USD with an approximate INR value.

## Add funds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & Credits">
    Go to **Settings → Billing & Credits**, or click **Add funds** on the Phone Numbers page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Pick **$25**, **$50**, **$100**, or **$200**, or enter a custom amount. The minimum is $10 and the maximum is $5,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Continue to payment**. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You're charged in INR at the live exchange rate, and your wallet stays in USD. 18% GST on services and estimated Razorpay fees are shown before you pay. Orato does not store card details.
</Info>

## What uses wallet credit

| Charge | How it's billed |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls and conversations** | Per billable minute, based on the assistant's transcriber, AI model, voice, and platform cost. See the live estimate in the assistant editor. |
| **Phone numbers** | Monthly price shown when you buy the number |

Track spend on the [Dashboard](/dashboard) (**Usage cost (USD)** and **Billable Minutes**) and per conversation on [Conversations](/conversations).

<Warning>
  Top up before launching a campaign so calls aren't interrupted by a low balance.
</Warning>

## Invoices

GST tax invoices for your purchases are listed under **Invoices** with invoice number, date, amount, and status. Invoice numbers follow the format `OR/sequence/FY` and are never reused.

Set the legal name, address, and GSTIN printed on invoices under [Workspace](/account/settings#workspace).
