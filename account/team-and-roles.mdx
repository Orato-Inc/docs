Go to **Settings → Team** to manage who has access to your workspace.

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    In **Settings → Team**, stay on the **Members** tab and click **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Enter the teammate's **Email** and choose a **Role**. The dialog shows what the role can and can't do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send invitation**. You can change the role later under **Members**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The members table shows each person's name, email, role, and status.

## Built-in roles

Roles sit on a ranked ladder. A **lower rank means more authority**, and a role can only manage members ranked below it.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Owner (rank 0)">
    **Sole account with full team control.** Best for the person who registered the workspace.

    **Can:**
    - Do everything an Admin can, plus manage Admins and transfer ownership
    - Invite or remove Admins, Staff, and Viewers
    - Change any member's role (except creating a second Owner)

    **Can't:**
    - Leave the Owner role without transferring ownership to an Admin (email OTP required)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Admin (rank 100)">
    **Full workspace access with peer limits.** Best for trusted leads who run the workspace day to day.

    **Can:**
    - Use billing, settings, integrations, and all operational features
    - Invite members as Admin, Staff, or Viewer
    - Change or remove Staff and Viewer members

    **Can't:**
    - Remove or change the Owner
    - Remove or change other Admins
    - Promote anyone to Owner (the Owner must transfer)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Staff (rank 200)">
    **Runs day-to-day operations.** Best for operators and agents who run campaigns and handle calls.

    **Can:**
    - Manage leads, campaigns (including start and pause), calls, assistants, meetings, and the knowledge base
    - View phone numbers, integrations, and dashboards

    **Can't:**
    - Access billing and the wallet
    - Invite or remove members, or edit roles
    - Manage API keys or connect integrations
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Viewer (rank 300)">
    **View-only access.** Best for people who review data without making changes.

    **Can:**
    - View leads, campaigns, call history, and dashboards

    **Can't:**
    - Create or update records, start campaigns, place calls, or change settings
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Click **View full permission list** on any role in the **Roles** tab for the complete breakdown.

## Custom roles

The Owner can add custom roles in the gaps between built-in ranks. For example, a role at rank 150 sits between Admin and Staff. Go to **Settings → Team → Roles** and click **Create role**.

## Transfer ownership

Ownership moves only through **Transfer ownership**, which is confirmed with an email OTP. Transfer to an existing Admin.
