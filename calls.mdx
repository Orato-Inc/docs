The **Calls** page (Call Management) lists every call your assistants make or receive.

<Frame>
  ![Call Management page](/images/app/calls.png)
</Frame>

## Summary cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Calls** | All calls, with change versus last week |
| **Completed** | Calls that finished normally, with change versus last week |
| **In Progress** | Calls happening now |
| **Failed** | Calls that couldn't connect or ended with an error |

## Find calls

Use the toolbar to narrow the list:

- **Search** by call, lead, or number
- **All status**: filter by call status
- **All direction**: inbound or outbound
- **All outcomes**: filter by outcome, such as Qualified or Not Interested
- **Date range**: pick a start and end date

Each row shows **Call details**, **Lead**, **Status**, **Duration**, **Outcome**, and **Date & time**. Change the page size at the bottom of the table.

## Export

Click **Export** to download the filtered list, for example to share results or import them into another tool.

## Make a call

Click **Make call** to call a single lead with an outbound assistant. See [Outbound calls](/calls/outbound).
