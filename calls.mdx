## Call types

Orato handles two types of calls:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Outbound Calls">
    Outbound calls are made **by your AI Assistant** to contacts in a Lead Batch, triggered through a Campaign.

    - Initiated automatically when a Campaign is running
    - Can also be triggered manually from the dashboard
    - Uses the phone number assigned to the assistant as the caller ID

    **Common use cases:** Lead qualification, appointment reminders, payment follow-ups, sales outreach
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Inbound Calls">
    Inbound calls are received **by your AI Assistant** when someone dials your Orato phone number.

    - Requires a phone number purchased from the Phone Numbers section
    - The assistant answers immediately - no hold time
    - Available 24/7, handles unlimited simultaneous calls

    **Common use cases:** Customer support, lead intake, appointment booking hotlines
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Real-time monitoring

The **Real-time** panel on the dashboard shows live call activity:

| Metric | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls/min** | Current call rate - how many calls are being made per minute |
| **Current Hour** | Number of calls in the current hour |
| **Active Calls** | Calls connected and in progress right now |
| **Today's Minutes** | Total billable minutes used today |

<Info>
  The real-time panel updates live. Use it to monitor active campaigns and ensure calls are progressing as expected.
</Info>

## Call outcomes

Every call is tagged with an outcome after it ends. View outcome breakdowns in the **Call Outcomes** chart:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualified** | Lead met the qualification criteria during the call |
| **Not Interested** | Lead explicitly declined |
| **No Answer** | Call rang but wasn't picked up |
| **Voicemail** | Call reached voicemail |
| **Busy** | Line was busy at time of call |
| **Failed** | Call could not be connected |
