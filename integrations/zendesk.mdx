## Overview

The Zendesk integration brings your entire Zendesk ticket queue into Orato. Your support agents can see open tickets, check a caller's history, and create new tickets mid-call — all from one place.

<Info>
  Zendesk integration is available on all Orato plans. You need an active Zendesk account with admin or agent access to connect it.
</Info>

## Connecting Zendesk to Orato

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In your Orato dashboard, go to **Integrations** from the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Zendesk">
    Locate the **Zendesk** card under the Ticketing category and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Zendesk Subdomain">
    You'll be asked for your Zendesk subdomain (e.g., `yourcompany` from `yourcompany.zendesk.com`) and an API token. You can generate an API token in Zendesk under **Admin → Channels → API**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Once you enter your credentials, Orato will verify the connection and begin syncing your tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Using It">
    Navigate to **Integrations → Zendesk** in Orato to see your ticket dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Make sure the Zendesk user whose API key you use has at least **Agent** role permissions. Tickets raised or updated by Orato will appear as actions from that user in your Zendesk audit log.
</Warning>

## The Zendesk Dashboard in Orato

Once connected, the Zendesk page inside Orato gives you a full ticket management interface.

### KPI Cards

At the top of the page, four metric cards show you at a glance:

| Card | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total** | Total number of tickets synced from your account |
| **Open** | All tickets with `open` or `new` status — needs attention |
| **Pending** | Tickets awaiting a response from the requester |
| **Solved** | Tickets marked solved or closed |

Clicking a card filters the table below to show only that ticket group.

### Ticket Table

The table displays your tickets with the following columns:

- **Ticket** — Subject line, a short preview of the description, and the ticket ID
- **Requester** — Name and email of the person who submitted the ticket
- **Status** — Color-coded badge: Open (red), New (purple), Pending (amber), Solved (green), Closed (gray)
- **Priority** — Urgent / High / Medium / Normal / Low with a colored indicator dot
- **Updated** — Relative time of the last update (e.g., "2h ago")
- **Open in Zendesk** — Direct link icon to open the ticket in your Zendesk dashboard

### Ticket Detail Panel

Click any row to open a side drawer with full ticket details:

- Status and priority chips
- Requester name, email, and phone
- Ticket ID and last updated timestamp
- Full ticket description (rendered HTML)
- A button to open the ticket directly in Zendesk

### Search

Use the search bar in the top-right of the table to filter tickets by subject, description snippet, requester name, or requester email. The result count updates as you type.

## Creating a New Ticket

Click the **New Ticket** button in the top-right of the Zendesk page to open the ticket creation modal.

Fill in:

- **Subject** — A short summary of the issue
- **Description** — Full details of what the caller reported
- **Priority** — Urgent, High, Medium, Normal, or Low
- **Requester email** — Who the ticket should be attributed to

After submission, the ticket appears in Zendesk immediately and the list in Orato refreshes automatically.

<Tip>
  During an active call, keep the Zendesk panel open in a secondary window or browser tab so you can raise a ticket without interrupting the conversation.
</Tip>

## Ticket Statuses Explained

| Status | Meaning in Zendesk |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Ticket just created, not yet assigned to an agent |
| **Open** | Assigned and being actively worked on |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the requester to respond |
| **Solved** | Agent has resolved the issue |
| **Closed** | Solved ticket that has been closed after a grace period |

---

## Data Sync Behavior

- Orato fetches tickets in pages of **25** at a time. Use the **Load more** button at the bottom of the table to fetch additional pages.
- Click **Refresh** in the top-right to pull the latest data from Zendesk at any point.
- Ticket data is not cached permanently in Orato — every refresh pulls live data from Zendesk.

## Disconnecting Zendesk

To remove the Zendesk integration, go to **Integrations** in your Orato dashboard, find the Zendesk card, and click **Disconnect**. This revokes Orato's access and removes the API credentials from our system.

<Note>
  Disconnecting does not delete any tickets from your Zendesk account. It only removes Orato's access to your Zendesk data.
</Note>
