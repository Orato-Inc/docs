Connect a helpdesk so that when a caller reports a problem, your assistant raises a ticket automatically. Your support team picks it up in the tool they already use.

| Tool | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **Zoho Desk** | Available |
| **Zendesk** | Available |
| **Freshdesk** | Request this integration |

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the helpdesk">
    Go to **Integrations**, find **Zoho Desk** or **Zendesk**, and click **Connect**. Complete the sign-in in the connection window.

    For Zoho Desk, you can optionally choose your account's **Domain Extension** (the region of your Zoho account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select it on your assistant">
    Open the assistant, go to the **Advanced** tab, and choose the tool under **Ticketing Integration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the assistant when to raise a ticket">
    Add guidance to the instructions:

    ```text
    If the caller reports a problem with an order, delivery, or payment,
    collect their name, order number, and a short description of the issue,
    then raise a support ticket and tell them the team will follow up.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Use **Talk** to report a test issue and check that the ticket appears in your helpdesk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Pair ticketing with **Transfer to Human** on the Advanced tab: raise a ticket for routine issues, and transfer urgent ones to a person.
</Tip>
