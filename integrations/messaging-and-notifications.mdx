## WhatsApp Business

Send follow-up messages and notifications to customers on WhatsApp, for example a brochure after a qualification call or a reminder before a meeting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Go to **Integrations**, find **WhatsApp Business**, and click **Connect**. Complete the authorization in the connection window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mention follow-ups in your prompt">
    Let the assistant offer a WhatsApp follow-up, for example `If they are interested, offer to send the brochure on WhatsApp.`
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  WhatsApp has its own rules for business messaging, including opt-in and approved templates. Make sure your use follows WhatsApp's business policies.
</Note>

## Slack

Get instant notifications in Slack for completed calls, new leads, and agent activity.

Go to **Integrations**, find **Slack**, click **Connect**, and choose the workspace to authorize.

## Gmail

Receive campaign reports, credit alerts, and lead notifications in your Gmail inbox.

Go to **Integrations**, find **Gmail**, click **Connect**, and sign in with the Google account that should receive alerts.

<Tip>
  Credit alerts in Gmail help you top up your [wallet](/account/billing) before a campaign runs out of funds.
</Tip>
