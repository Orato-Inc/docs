Integrations let your assistants work with the tools your team already uses. Go to **Integrations** in the sidebar. Once connected, each integration gets its own dedicated workspace.

<Frame>
  ![Integrations page](/images/app/integrations.png)
</Frame>

## Available integrations

Integrations marked **Connect** are available now. Others show **Request this integration** so you can tell the Orato team you need them.

| Integration | Category | What it does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Zoho Desk** | Ticketing | Raise and track support tickets directly from calls in real time | Connect |
| **Zendesk** | Ticketing | Create and manage support tickets without leaving Orato | Connect |
| **Freshdesk** | Ticketing | Manage support tickets during calls | Request |
| **HubSpot CRM** | CRM | Sync call data, leads, and contacts with your CRM | Request |
| **Salesforce** | CRM | Push call outcomes and leads into Salesforce from every conversation | Request |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Communication | Send follow-up messages and notifications to customers on WhatsApp | Connect |
| **Slack** | Productivity | Get notifications for completed calls, new leads, and agent activity | Connect |
| **Gmail** | Productivity | Receive campaign reports, credit alerts, and lead notifications in your inbox | Connect |
| **Google Calendar** | Productivity | Schedule follow-up meetings and sync call appointments | Connect |
| **Google Sheets** | Productivity | Export call data and leads to a live spreadsheet | Request |
| **Microsoft Teams** | Productivity | Get call and lead alerts in Teams channels | Request |
| **Zapier** | Productivity | Connect Orato to thousands of other apps | Request |

Use the search box or the **Ticketing**, **CRM**, **Communication**, and **Productivity** filters to find an integration. The **Docs** link on each card opens that vendor's documentation.

## Connect an integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    On the integration's card, click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    A secure connection window opens (powered by Nango). Enter any details the tool asks for and sign in to authorize Orato.

    <Frame>
      ![Connection window for Zoho Desk](/images/app/integrations-connect.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The integration shows as connected, and the counter at the top of the page updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only Owners and Admins can connect integrations. Staff and Viewers can see them. See [Team and roles](/account/team-and-roles).
</Note>
