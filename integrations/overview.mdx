## How Integrations Work in Orato

When you connect an integration:

1. **Authentication**: You authorize Orato to access your third-party account (OAuth or API key, depending on the provider).
2. **Sync**: Orato fetches your existing data (tickets, contacts, leads) and keeps it up to date in real time.
3. **In-call actions**: Your agents can trigger actions (like creating a ticket) directly from the call interface without navigating away.
4. **Post-call automation**: After a call ends, Orato can automatically push data to your connected tools based on outcomes and dispositions.

<Note>
  Orato does not store credentials from third-party platforms in plain text. All tokens are encrypted at rest. You can revoke access to any integration from your dashboard at any time.
</Note>

## CRM Integrations

The **CRM Integration** section gives your team a live view of support tickets from Zoho Desk and Zendesk, right inside Orato. Agents can:

- View all open, pending, and resolved tickets
- Filter and search by keyword, status, or priority
- Open a ticket's full details in a side drawer
- Create new tickets on behalf of a caller, without leaving the call

<Tip>
  If you handle high inbound call volumes for customer support, connecting Zendesk or Zoho Desk is one of the highest-ROI things you can do. Agents stop context-switching, and tickets get raised faster with better accuracy.
</Tip>
