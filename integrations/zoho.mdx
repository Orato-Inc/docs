## Overview

The Zoho Desk integration connects your Zoho Desk account to Orato, so your support team can handle tickets directly from within the platform. No more tab-switching mid-call, view a caller's ticket history, check open issues, or raise a new ticket in seconds.

## Connecting Zoho Desk to Orato

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In your Orato dashboard, go to **Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Zoho Desk">
    Locate the **Zoho Desk** card under the Ticketing category and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Zoho">
    You'll be redirected to Zoho's OAuth authorization page. Sign in with your Zoho account and grant Orato the required permissions to read and write tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Organization">
    If your Zoho account has multiple organizations, select the one whose tickets you want to sync into Orato.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're Connected">
    Orato will start syncing your Zoho Desk tickets. Navigate to **Integrations → Zoho Desk** to see your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Ensure the Zoho Desk user you authorize has appropriate agent permissions. Tickets created via Orato will appear in Zoho as actions from this user.
</Warning>

## The Zoho Desk Dashboard in Orato

### KPI Cards

Four summary cards at the top give you an instant overview:

| Card | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total** | All tickets are currently synced from Zoho Desk |
| **Open** | Tickets with `open` status, actively being worked on |
| **On Hold** | Tickets paused, waiting for an internal team action |
| **Resolved** | Tickets marked as resolved or closed |

Clicking any card filters the ticket table to that group.

### Ticket Table

Each row in the ticket table contains:

- **Ticket**: Subject, a short preview of the ticket body, and the unique ticket ID
- **Contact**: Name and email of the requester
- **Status**: Color-coded badge (Open, On Hold, Pending, Resolved, Closed)
- **Priority**: High / Medium / Low with a colored dot indicator
- **Updated**: Relative timestamp (e.g., "3h ago")
- **Open in Zoho Desk**: Direct link to the ticket in your Zoho portal

### Ticket Detail Panel

Click any ticket row to open a slide-in panel showing:

- Status and priority at a glance
- Contact details: name, email, phone
- Full ticket description
- Ticket ID and last updated time
- **Open in Zoho Desk** button for direct access

### Search

The search bar filters tickets in real time across subject, description, contact name, and email. The visible result count updates as you type.

## Creating a New Ticket

Click **New Ticket** in the top-right to open the ticket creation modal. Fill in:

- **Subject**: A brief, descriptive title for the issue
- **Description**: What the caller reported, in as much detail as needed
- **Priority**: High, Medium, or Low
- **Contact email**: Who the ticket belongs to

Once submitted, the ticket is created instantly in Zoho Desk, and the table refreshes.

<Tip>
  If a caller has already raised a ticket previously, search their name or phone number before creating a new one to avoid duplicate tickets. Zoho Desk agents can then merge them if needed.
</Tip>

## Ticket Statuses in Zoho Desk

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | The ticket is active and assigned to an agent |
| **On Hold** | The ticket is paused, typically waiting for internal review |
| **Pending** | Awaiting a response from the requester |
| **Resolved** | The agent has marked the issue as resolved |
| **Closed** | The ticket has been fully closed after resolution |

## Differences from Zendesk

If you're deciding between the two integrations:

| Feature | Zoho Desk | Zendesk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status: On Hold | ✅ Yes | ❌ No (uses Pending) |
| Priority levels | High / Medium / Low | Urgent / High / Medium / Normal / Low |
| OAuth flow | ✅ OAuth 2.0 | API key |
| Column label | "Contact" | "Requester" |

Both integrations support the same core features: live ticket view, search, ticket detail panel, ticket creation, and refresh.

## Data Sync Behavior

- Tickets load in pages of **25**. Click **Load more** at the bottom of the table to fetch the next page.
- Hit **Refresh** anytime to pull the latest ticket state from Zoho Desk.
- Data is fetched live on demand; Orato does not store a permanent local copy of your Zoho Desk tickets.

---

## Disconnecting Zoho Desk

Go to **Integrations** in your dashboard, find the Zoho Desk card, and click **Disconnect**. This revokes Orato's OAuth token and removes the connection.

<Note>
  Disconnecting Zoho Desk from Orato does not affect your Zoho Desk account or any tickets created through Orato. It only removes the live sync.
</Note>
