Connecting Google Calendar keeps your Orato meetings and your calendar in sync.

## What you get

- Meetings sync both ways between Orato and Google Calendar
- Real-time availability checking to prevent double bookings
- All meetings in one dashboard
- Calendar invitations sent to clients
- Notifications and reminders

## Connect

You can connect from either place:

- **Meetings** page: click **Connect Google Calendar**.
- **Integrations** page: click **Connect** on the **Google Calendar** card.

Sign in with the Google account you use for client meetings and approve access.

## Use it

Once connected, meetings you schedule on the [Meetings](/meetings) page appear in Google Calendar, and today's meetings show on the [Dashboard](/dashboard).

<Tip>
  Use a shared team calendar if several people take meetings booked from Orato calls.
</Tip>
