## Overview

**Manual Calls** is Orato's browser-based calling feature that lets your agents make outbound calls directly from the Orato dashboard, no external phone, SIP app, or softphone needed. Every manual call gets a recording, a full transcript, an AI summary, and an outcome so your team has a complete paper trail.

<Frame>
  ![New Manual Calls](/images/new-manual-calls.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  Manual Calls uses your browser's microphone and WebRTC to place calls. Make sure your browser has microphone permissions enabled for `app.tryorato.com`.
</Info>

## Navigating the Manual Calls Page

Go to **Manual Calls** in your Orato sidebar to open the Manual Calls dashboard.

### Metric Cards

The top row shows four live metrics:

| Card | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Calls** | Total manual calls made on your account |
| **Completed** | Calls that connected and completed successfully (current page) |
| **Minutes Used** | Total minutes consumed across calls on the current page |
| **Quota Remaining** | Your remaining calling quota (e.g., `43 / 60 minutes`) |

### Filters and Search

Use the toolbar below the metrics to narrow your call list:

- **Search**: Filter by phone number or contact name
- **Status filter**: All, Completed, In Progress, Failed, No Answer
- **Outcome filter**: All, Qualified, Not Interested, Callback, No Answer, Voicemail

### Call Table

Each row in the call table shows:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | Lead name (if matched) and phone number |
| **Status** | Completed / In Progress / Failed / No Answer |
| **Outcome** | The disposition set for this call |
| **Duration** | Call length in `m:ss` format. Shows `N/A` for unanswered calls |
| **Date & Time** | When the call started |
| **Actions (⋯)** | Options to play the recording, view the transcript, or download |

## Making a Manual Call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dialer">
    Click the **Open Dialer** button in the top-right of the Manual Calls page. The dialer panel slides open on the right side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a Phone Number">
    Type the number you want to call in E.164 format (e.g., `+919876543210`). Or, use the **Phonebook** to find an existing lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Caller ID">
    Make sure your active phone number is selected in the top bar of the Orato dashboard before calling. This is the number that will be shown to the recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Call">
    Click the **Call** button in the dialer. Your browser will use your microphone to connect to the call through Orato's telephony infrastructure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the Call">
    Click **End Call** when the conversation is complete. Orato will process the recording and transcript in the background.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If the dialer says "No microphone found" or the call fails immediately, check that your browser has microphone access: go to **Site Settings** in your browser and ensure microphone permission is set to **Allow** for `app.tryorato.com`.
</Warning>

## Using the Phonebook

The **Phonebook** is your directory of saved leads and contacts within Orato.

1. Click **Phonebook** in the top-right of the Manual Calls page
2. Browse or search for a contact by name or number
3. Click the **Call** icon next to any lead to auto-populate their number in the dialer and initiate the call.

<Tip>
  The Phonebook pulls from your **Leads** database in Orato. If a contact isn't showing up, check that they've been added as a lead with a valid phone number.
</Tip>

## Viewing Call Recordings and Transcripts

After a call completes, click the **⋯** menu on the call row to access:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Play Recording">
    Opens an in-app audio player modal with the call recording. The player shows call details (contact name, number, date, duration) and lets you play or pause directly in the browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="View Transcript & Summary">
    Opens the **Call Transcript & Summary** modal with the AI-generated summary and full turn-by-turn transcript. Supports keyword search and highlighting, see [Transcript & Search](/calls/transcript-search) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Download Recording">
    Downloads the call recording as an `.mp3` file directly to your machine. The download requires authentication and happens securely through Orato's backend.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Call Statuses

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Completed** | Call connected and ended normally |
| **In Progress** | Call is currently active |
| **Failed** | Call could not connect due to a technical error |
| **No Answer** | The recipient did not pick up |

---

## Call Outcomes

Outcomes are set automatically based on the call or can be updated manually:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualified** | Lead showed interest and meets your criteria |
| **Not Interested** | Lead declined or explicitly not interested |
| **Callback** | Lead asked to be called back at a later time |
| **No Answer** | The call rang, but was not picked up |
| **Voicemail** | Call reached voicemail |
| **Pending** | Outcome not yet determined |

## Quota and Minute Tracking

Manual Calls consume from your organization's **calling minute quota**. The **Quota Remaining** card shows your current balance in real time (e.g., `43 / 60 minutes`).

If your quota runs out:

- The dialer will show a warning and prevent new calls
- Contact your account admin or [support@tryorato.com](mailto:support@tryorato.com) to top up your quota

<Note>
  Quota is tracked per organization, not per user. All agents on your account share the same minute pool.
</Note>

## Pagination

The call table loads **20 calls per page** by default. Use the pagination controls at the bottom of the table to navigate between pages or change the page size (20, 50, or 100 rows per page).

The table also shows a count like "Showing 1–20 of 143 calls," so you always know where you are.
