  ![Voice tab](/images/app/assistant-voice.png)

## Voice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a provider">
    Choose **Cartesia**, **ElevenLabs**, **Sarvam**, or **Smallest AI**. The cost estimate at the top of the page updates when you switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model and voice">
    **Model** and **Voice** are optional. If you leave them, Orato uses the provider's default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Click **Preview** to hear the selected voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the speed">
    Drag the **Speed** slider. The preview replays automatically when you release it, and **Talk** uses the same speed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Providers compared

| Provider | Example models | Speed range | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cartesia** | Sonic 3 | 0.6x to 1.5x | Natural, low-latency English and Indian voices. Default choice. |
| **ElevenLabs** | Turbo v2.5, Flash 2.5 | 0.7x to 1.2x | Highly expressive voices |
| **Sarvam** | Bulbul v3 | 0.5x to 2x | Hindi, Indian English, and Indian regional languages |
| **Smallest AI** | Lightning v3.1 Pro | 0.5x to 2x | The lowest voice cost per minute |

<Tip>
  Match the voice to your audience. For Indian callers, try a Cartesia or Sarvam voice with an Indian accent. Keep speed between 0.9x and 1.1x for phone calls; very fast speech is hard to follow on a phone line.
</Tip>

## Languages

### Primary language

The language the agent starts the conversation in. Options include:

- English (US)
- English (India)
- Hindi
- Tamil
- Telugu
- Marathi
- Gujarati
- Bengali

The primary language also guides which [transcriber](/assistants/advanced#transcriber) Orato picks for you.

### Auto switch language

Turn on **Auto switch language** to let the agent follow the caller. If the caller switches language mid-call, the agent switches too.

<Note>
  For the best results in a non-English language, also translate your instructions with **Translate prompt** on the [Prompt](/assistants/prompt#translate-prompt) tab and choose a voice that supports that language.
</Note>
