The **Advanced** tab holds call controls and the actions your assistant can take during a conversation. Every field is optional.

<Frame>
  ![Advanced tab](/images/app/assistant-advanced.png)
</Frame>

## Transcriber

Orato picks the speech-to-text model automatically from your [languages](/assistants/voice-and-language#languages). The default for English is **Flux English (Recommended)**.

Override it only if you need a specific model. Click **Change**, then choose:

| Provider | Models |
| --- | --- |
| **Deepgram** | Flux English (Recommended), Flux Multilingual |
| **Sarvam AI** | Saaras v4 Realtime, Saaras v3 |

Click **Reset to recommended** to go back to automatic selection.

<Tip>
  Use **Sarvam AI** for Hindi and Indian regional languages, and **Flux Multilingual** when callers mix languages.
</Tip>

## Call limits

| Field | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Max Duration (s)** | 300 | Longest a single call can last, in seconds |
| **Silence Timeout (s)** | 30 | How long the assistant waits in silence before ending the call |
| **Response Delay (s)** | 0.0 | Extra pause before the assistant replies |

<Note>
  Raise **Max Duration** for support or consultation calls that run longer than five minutes. Keep it lower for quick qualification calls to control cost.
</Note>

## End call phrases

The assistant hangs up if the caller says one of these phrases. Type a phrase and press **Enter** to add it, for example `bye`, `not interested`, or `don't call me again`.

## Enable recording

On by default. Turn it off if you don't want calls recorded.

<Warning>
  Recording rules differ by country. Make sure you inform callers and follow the laws that apply to you.
</Warning>

## Transfer to Human

Enter a phone number in E.164 format, such as `+14155552671`. When the caller asks for a human, the assistant bridges the call to this number.

Add a line to your instructions so the hand-off sounds natural:

```text
If the caller asks for a person or you cannot help after two tries,
say "Let me connect you with someone from our team" and transfer the call.
```

<Warning>
  Make sure the transfer number is staffed during calling hours. A transfer to an unanswered line is a poor experience for the caller.
</Warning>

## Ticketing integration

When a caller reports an issue, the assistant raises a ticket in the tool you select here, such as Zoho Desk or Zendesk. Connect the tool on the [Integrations](/integrations/ticketing) page first; until then the field shows **No integrations connected yet**.

## Knowledge documents

Choose documents from your [knowledge base](/knowledge-base) for this assistant to search during calls and widget chats. Only documents with a ready status appear. Upload files or import websites under **Knowledge Base** first.

<Tip>
  You can also refer to tools and variables in your instructions with the `@` shortcut on the [Prompt](/assistants/prompt#instructions) tab.
</Tip>
