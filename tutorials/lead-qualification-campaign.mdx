title: "Run a lead qualification campaign"
description: "Import leads, build a qualifier assistant from a template, and call the whole batch with a campaign."

---

In this tutorial you'll call a list of property enquiries, qualify each lead on budget, location, and timeline, and see the results on the dashboard. Swap in your own industry as you go.

**You'll need:** a wallet balance, a phone number for caller ID, and a CSV of leads.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your leads">
    Create a CSV with `name` and `phone` columns. Add any extra columns you find useful, such as `notes` or `city`.

    ```csv
    name,phone,notes,city
    Rahul Sharma,+919876543210,Enquired about 2BHK,Pune
    Ananya Iyer,+919812345678,Wants a villa,Bengaluru
    ```

    Go to **Leads → Create batch → Batch with CSV**, name the batch `Property enquiries - October`, upload the file, and click **Create Batch with CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the assistant from a template">
    Go to **AI Assistants** and click **Real Estate Lead Qualifier**. The first message and instructions are filled in for you.

    - Set **Agent name** to `Priya`.
    - In the instructions, replace the generic company and city with your own.
    - Add your definition of qualified, for example `A lead is qualified if they plan to buy within 3 months with a budget above ₹80 lakh.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the voice">
    On the **Voice** tab, choose **Cartesia**, preview a few Indian English voices, and set **Primary language** to **English (India)**. Turn on **Auto switch language** in case leads reply in Hindi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings">
    On the **Settings** tab:

    - **Role**: Outbound
    - **Outbound caller ID**: your Indian number
    - **AI Brain**: Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5
    - **Who speaks first?**: Agent speaks first
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set call controls">
    On the **Advanced** tab:

    - **Max Duration**: 300 seconds is plenty for qualification.
    - **End Call Phrases**: add `not interested` and `don't call me`.
    - **Transfer to Human**: your sales team's number, for hot leads who want to talk now.
    - **Knowledge documents**: attach your project brochure or price list.

    Click **Create Assistant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before you launch">
    Use **Talk** to play a few leads: an interested buyer, a busy person, and someone who isn't interested. Refine the prompt until all three go well.

    Then create a small batch with your own number and run a one-lead campaign to hear a real call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the campaign">
    Go to **Campaigns → + Create campaign**:

    - **Campaign Name**: `Property enquiries - October`
    - **Lead Batch**: the batch you created
    - **Select Assistant**: Priya
    - **Call rate**: 1 to 2 calls per minute
    - **Schedule**: Start Immediately, or schedule for business hours

    Click **Create Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track results">
    - Watch progress on the **Campaigns** page.
    - See outcomes (Connected, No Answer, Callback, Not Interested, Qualified) on the **Dashboard**.
    - Read summaries on **Conversations** and schedule site visits for qualified leads on **Meetings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
