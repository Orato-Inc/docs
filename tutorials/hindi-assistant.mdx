In this tutorial you'll build an outbound assistant that follows up on loan applications in Hindi.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from a template">
    Go to **AI Assistants** and click **NBFC Loan Follow-up**. Set the **Agent name**, for example `Neha`, and replace the lender details in the instructions with your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the language">
    On the **Voice** tab:

    - **Primary language**: Hindi
    - **Auto switch language**: on, so the assistant follows callers who reply in English
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Hindi voice">
    Select **Sarvam** (model **Bulbul v3**) and preview the voices. Pick one that sounds natural for your audience and keep the speed close to 1.0x.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate the instructions">
    On the **Prompt** tab, click **Translate prompt**. Orato rewrites the instructions in Devanagari with a natural, spoken tone. Placeholders like `{customer_name}` and your section structure stay as they are.

    Review the result, then update the **First message** in Hindi, for example:

    ```text
    नमस्ते {{customer_name}} जी, मैं नेहा बोल रही हूँ। आपके लोन आवेदन के बारे में दो मिनट बात कर सकते हैं?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the transcriber">
    On the **Advanced** tab, Orato picks a transcriber from your languages. To set it yourself, click **Change** and choose **Sarvam AI → Saaras v4 Realtime**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the AI model">
    On the **Settings** tab, set **Role** to **Outbound** and choose a caller ID. Try **Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5** first, then compare with **Sarvam AI → Sarvam 105B**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with native speakers">
    Use **Talk** and ask colleagues who speak Hindi to try it. Listen for unnatural phrasing, wrong number formats, and how it handles Hinglish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Ask the assistant to say amounts the way callers expect, for example in lakhs.
- Keep compliance language (fees, interest, consent) in **Guardrails**, and review the translated version carefully.
- See [Multilingual assistants](/multilingual) for other Indian languages.
