An inbound assistant answers calls to one of your Orato numbers. It never misses a call, works around the clock, and hands off to a person when needed.

## Set up inbound calling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy a number">
    Go to [Phone numbers](/phone-numbers) and buy a US number, or an Indian number after compliance approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an inbound assistant">
    Create an assistant (the **Clinic Appointment Scheduler** or **E-commerce Customer Support** template is a good start). On the [Settings](/assistants/settings) tab:

    - Set **Role** to **Inbound** (Answer incoming calls).
    - Choose your number under **Inbound phone number**.
    - Consider **Agent waits for user** under **Who speaks first?** if callers tend to greet first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the greeting">
    Add a **First message** that tells callers where they've reached:

    ```text
    Thank you for calling Acme Clinic. This is Asha, how can I help you today?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a human fallback">
    On the [Advanced](/assistants/advanced#transfer-to-human) tab, add a **Transfer to Human** number for callers who ask for a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach knowledge">
    Upload FAQs, opening hours, and policies to the [knowledge base](/knowledge-base) and select them under **Knowledge documents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Call the number from your own phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inbound outcomes

The [Dashboard](/dashboard) tracks inbound calls by outcome:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Answered by AI** | The assistant handled the call |
| **Escalated to Human** | The call was transferred to your team |
| **Missed** | The call wasn't answered |
| **Voicemail Left** | The caller left a voicemail |
| **Callback Requested** | The caller asked to be called back |

The dashboard also shows the **Inbound Answer Rate** and **AI Handle Rate**.

## Tips

- Keep the greeting short. Callers want to state their reason quickly.
- Give clear rules for when to transfer, such as billing disputes or upset callers.
- Connect a [ticketing integration](/integrations/ticketing) so reported issues become tickets automatically.
