## Overview

Every call processed by Orato produces a full conversation transcript and an AI-generated summary. The **Transcript & Search** feature lets you search for any keyword inside these, either within a single call or across your entire call history, so you can find exactly what was said, by whom, and when.

---

## Inside a Single Call: The Transcript Viewer

When you open a call from the **Calls** page (by clicking the eye icon on any row), the **Call Transcript & Summary** modal opens.

This modal has two panels side by side:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Call Summary" icon="file-lines">
    An AI-generated summary of the call, key points discussed, outcome, and next steps. Useful for quick reviews without reading the full transcript.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Conversation Transcript" icon="comments">
    The full turn-by-turn transcript of the call. Each message is labeled, **AI Assistant** or **Customer**, with the exact words spoken.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Keyword Search Inside a Transcript

At the top of the transcript modal, there is a **search bar**. Type any keyword and Orato will:

1. Scan both the **Call Summary** and the **Conversation Transcript** simultaneously
2. **Highlight every match** in amber/yellow, so they're visually easy to spot
3. Scroll you to the first match automatically

<Frame>
  ![New Search](/images/new-search.png)
</Frame>

### How to Use It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Call">
    Go to **Calls** in your Orato dashboard and click the eye icon (👁) on the call you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type Your Keyword">
    In the search bar at the top of the modal (e.g., type `hospital`, `pricing`, `refund`, `follow-up`The matches appear highlighted immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Highlights">
    All matched words are highlighted in both the summary and the transcript, and you can see context around each match easily.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy if Needed">
    Use the **Copy** buttons next to each panel to copy the summary or transcript to the clipboard. **Copy All** copies both at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  This is especially useful for quality assurance; managers can search for keywords like "complaint", "cancel", "competitor", or "price" to quickly audit how those topics were handled in calls.
</Tip>

## Cross-Call Search: Finding Calls by Keyword

You can also search for keywords **from the main Call Management page**, before even opening a specific call. This searches across all call summaries and transcripts in your account.

<Frame>
  ![New Call Search](/images/new-call-search.png)
</Frame>

### How It Works

1. Go to the **Calls** page in your Orato dashboard
2. Type a keyword in the top search bar (e.g., `Hospital`, `callback`, `interested`)
3. The call list **filters in real time**, and only calls where that keyword appears in the transcript or summary remain visible
4. Open any filtered call to see the highlighted matches inside the transcript

<Info>
  Cross-call search helps you find patterns across hundreds of calls instantly. For example, searching "not interested" shows you every call where a prospect expressed disinterest, useful for refining your assistant's objection-handling prompt.
</Info>

## Call Details Available in the Transcript Modal

The transcript modal also surfaces key call metadata at the top:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Call Type** | Inbound or Outbound |
| **Duration** | Total call length (e.g., `0:15`) |
| **Outcome** | Disposition set during or after the call (e.g., `CALLBACK`, `QUALIFIED`) |
| **Call Date & Time** | Exact timestamp of when the call occurred |

## Tips for Better Search Results

- Use **single keywords** rather than full sentences for broader matches (e.g., `hospital` instead of `hospital name`)
- The search is **case-insensitive** - `CALLBACK` and `callback` return the same results
- If you are searching in the transcript modal, both the summary and the transcript are searched simultaneously
- From the Calls page, the search filters calls where the keyword appears **anywhere** in the transcript or summary

<Note>
  Transcript data is only available for calls that have completed processing. Calls still being transcribed may not appear in search results immediately. Typically, transcripts are ready within a few seconds of call completion.
</Note>
