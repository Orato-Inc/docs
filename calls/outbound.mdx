Outbound calls are made by assistants with the **Outbound** role. There are two ways to start them.

| Method | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Make call** on the Calls page | One lead, right now: a follow-up, a test, or a hot lead |
| **Campaigns** | Every lead in a batch, at a controlled call rate |

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an outbound assistant">
    On the assistant's [Settings](/assistants/settings) tab, set **Role** to **Outbound** and **Who speaks first?** to **Agent speaks first**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the caller ID">
    Buy a number on [Phone numbers](/phone-numbers) and choose it under **Outbound caller ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a strong first message">
    The first few seconds decide whether people stay on the line. Say who you are, why you're calling, and ask permission:

    ```text
    Hi {{customer_name}}, this is Priya from Acme Realty. You enquired about homes in Pune last week. Is now a good time for two minutes?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add leads">
    Create a [lead batch](/leads) with names and phone numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Call a single lead

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calls">
    Click **Calls** in the sidebar, then **Make call**.

    <Frame>
      ![Make Outbound Call dialog](/images/app/calls-make-call.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the lead">
    Type a name or phone number to search your leads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the assistant">
    Choose the AI assistant for this call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate">
    Click **Initiate Call**. The call is logged and tracked automatically on the Calls page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Call a batch

Use a [campaign](/campaigns) to call every lead in a batch, immediately or on a schedule.

## After the call

- See status, duration, and outcome on [Calls](/calls).
- Read the summary and billing on [Conversations](/conversations).
- Schedule a follow-up on [Meetings](/meetings).
