## What are Campaigns?

A Campaign is an automated outbound calling operation. You connect a **Lead Batch** to an **AI Assistant** and Orato calls every contact in the batch automatically. Track progress, call outcomes, and qualified leads from the Campaigns dashboard.

---

## Campaigns dashboard

The Campaigns page shows an overview of all your campaigns:

| Stat | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Campaigns** | All campaigns ever created |
| **Active** | Currently running campaigns |
| **Total Calls** | Total calls made across all campaigns |
| **Qualified Leads** | Leads marked as qualified by your assistant |

Each campaign row shows: name, status, progress bar, start time, results, and actions (view, edit, delete).

### Campaign statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Running` | Campaign is actively making calls |
| `Completed` | All contacts in the batch have been called |
| `Cancelled` | Campaign was manually stopped |

## Creating a campaign

Click **\+ Create Campaign** from the Campaigns page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Campaign Details">
    Fill in:

    - **Campaign Name**: a descriptive name (e.g. _"May Outreach - Real Estate Leads"_)
    - **Lead Batch**: select the batch of leads to call (create one first in the Leads section if needed)
    - **Description**: optional notes about the campaign's purpose and goals
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select AI Assistant">
    Choose which AI Assistant will handle the calls. The assistant you select will make every call in this campaign.

    <Tip>
      Make sure your selected assistant has instructions tailored for the type of leads in your batch. A lead qualification assistant will perform very differently from a support assistant.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Lead Filters (optional)">
    Narrow down which leads in the batch get called:

    - **Lead Status**: filter by lead status (e.g. only call new leads)
    - **Lead Score**: filter by score (e.g. only call high-score leads)

    Leave both empty to call all leads in the batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Campaign Settings">
    - **Call Rate (calls per minute)**: default is 5. Recommended range: **3–8 calls per minute** to avoid spam detection by carriers.
    - **Schedule**:
      - **Start Immediately**: campaign begins as soon as you click Create
      - **Schedule for Later**: set a specific date and time to start

    <Warning>
      Setting the call rate above 8 calls per minute may trigger spam filters. Stick to 3–8 for best delivery rates.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Create Campaign**. If you chose Start Immediately, your AI Assistant begins calling right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monitoring a campaign

Click on any campaign to see its live progress:

- **Progress bar**: shows how many contacts have been called vs total
- **Results**: breakdown of call outcomes (connected, no answer, failed, qualified)
- **Individual call logs**: click through to see each call's details and transcript
