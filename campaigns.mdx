A campaign connects a lead batch to an outbound assistant and calls each lead for you.

<Frame>
  ![Campaigns page](/images/app/campaigns.png)
</Frame>

## Before you start

<Check>An assistant with the **Outbound** role. See [Settings](/assistants/settings#role).</Check>
<Check>An **Outbound caller ID** set on that assistant. See [Phone numbers](/phone-numbers).</Check>
<Check>A lead batch. See [Leads](/leads).</Check>
<Check>Enough wallet balance. See [Billing and credits](/account/billing).</Check>

## Create a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create campaign">
    Go to **Campaigns** and click **+ Create campaign**.

    <Frame>
      ![Create New Campaign dialog](/images/app/campaigns-create.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaign details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name**, pick the **Lead Batch**, and optionally describe the campaign's purpose and goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the assistant">
    Search for and choose the AI assistant that will make the calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the call rate">
    Enter the **Call rate (calls per minute)**. Orato limits outbound cadence to 1 to 2 calls per minute for cost and carrier compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Choose **Start Immediately** or **Schedule for Later** and pick a start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create Campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monitor campaigns

The Campaigns page shows:

| Stat | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total campaigns** | All campaigns you've created |
| **Active** | Campaigns currently running |
| **Total calls** | Calls placed across all campaigns |
| **Qualified leads** | Leads marked qualified from campaign outcomes |

Each row shows the campaign's **Status**, **Progress**, **Start Time**, **Results**, and **Created** date, with actions to manage it. Filter by status or search by name.

Individual calls appear on the [Calls](/calls) page, and outcomes roll up to the [Dashboard](/dashboard).

## Call outcomes

Outbound calls are grouped into these outcomes:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The lead answered |
| **No Answer** | The call wasn't picked up |
| **Callback** | The lead asked to be called back |
| **Not Interested** | The lead declined |
| **Qualified** | The lead met your qualification criteria |

<Tip>
  Tell your assistant what "qualified" means in its instructions, for example `A lead is qualified if they plan to buy within 3 months.` Clear criteria give you cleaner outcome data.
</Tip>

## Best practices

- **Test first.** Run a small batch of internal numbers before calling real leads.
- **Call at sensible hours** in your leads' time zone, and respect do-not-call requests.
- **Keep batches focused.** One audience and one goal per campaign makes results easier to read.
- **Review conversations** from the first calls and refine the prompt before scaling up.
