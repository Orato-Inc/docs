## Overview

The Slack integration brings Orato's activity feed into the channels your team already lives in. Connect your workspace once, pick a default channel, choose what you want Orato to post about, and your team stays in the loop, without anyone needing to check the dashboard.

<Info>
  Slack integration is available on all Orato plans. You'll need admin access to the Slack workspace you want to connect to or permission from your Slack admin.
</Info>

## Connecting Your Slack Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Slack settings">
    In your Orato dashboard, go to **CRM Integration → Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Authorize Orato on Slack">
    Hit the **Authorize Orato on Slack** button. A secure authorization window will open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a workspace and approve">
    Choose the Slack workspace you want to connect and grant Orato the permissions it needs:

    | Permission | Why is it needed |
    | --- | --- |
    | `chat:write` | To post notification messages to channels |
    | `channels:read` | To list your public channels so you can pick one |
    | `channels:join` | To join the selected channel before posting |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Once authorized, the status badge updates to **Connected,** and the channel selector and notification preferences appear below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Orato can only post messages to the channel you select; it cannot read your existing Slack messages, DMs, or any other channel activity. Your workspace credentials are stored encrypted in your organisation's secure vault.
</Note>

## Choosing a Default Channel

After connecting, you'll see a **Default notification channel** dropdown. This is the channel where all enabled Orato notifications will be posted.

- Only public channels are listed
- You can change the channel at any time without disconnecting
- Orato automatically joins the channel before posting if it hasn't already

<Tip>
  Create a dedicated `#orato-alerts` channel so notifications don't clutter your general or team channels. It keeps things clean and easy to search later.
</Tip>

---

## Choosing Your Notifications

Once a channel is selected, a notification grid appears. Toggle each event type on or off for your workspace.

| Notification | What triggers it |
| --- | --- |
| **Low credits alert** | Credits fall below your defined threshold |
| **System errors** | Critical runtime failures and exceptions |
| **Campaign reports** | Summary stats are posted when a campaign finishes |
| **Qualified leads** | A lead meets your qualification criteria |
| **New meetings booked** | An appointment or consultation gets scheduled |
| **Campaign started** | A campaign goes live and begins calling |
| **Campaign stopped** | A campaign is manually stopped or cancelled |

The counter next to the section title shows how many are currently active (e.g. `4 / 7 active`).

---

## Setting a Low Credit Threshold

If **the low credits alert** is enabled, you can configure when it fires.

- Enter a dollar amount in the threshold input
- Orato posts a Slack message when the credits drop to a value below
- The minimum allowed value is **\$2.00**

## Saving Your Preferences

When you're happy with your channel and notification selections, click **Save preferences**. Changes take effect immediately; the next time a triggered event happens, the message goes straight to Slack.

To undo unsaved changes, click **Discard changes** to reload your last saved state.

---

## Disconnecting Slack

To remove the integration, click **Disconnect workspace**. This stops all Slack notifications and clears the stored credentials from your vault.

You can reconnect at any time. Your previous notification preferences won't be remembered after disconnecting, so you'll need to reconfigure them when you reconnect.

## Troubleshooting

**I authorized the workspace, but nothing is appearing in Slack**

Make sure you've selected a default channel and clicked **Save preferences**. Notifications won't post until both steps are complete.

**My channel isn't showing up in the dropdown**

Only public channels are listed. If the channel you want is private, make it public first — or create a dedicated public channel for Orato alerts.

**I see "Failed to initialize Slack connection."**

The authorization session may have timed out. Click **Authorize Orato on Slack** again and complete the flow without closing the pop-up window.

**The connected status isn't updating after I approved**

Wait a few seconds and refresh the page. If it still shows disconnected, click **Disconnect** and try reconnecting.
