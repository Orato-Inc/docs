## Overview

The Gmail integration lets Orato send you email notifications whenever something important happens, a campaign wraps up, a lead qualifies, credits run low, or an error needs your attention. You connect once, pick what you want to hear about, and Orato handles the rest.

<Info>
  Gmail integration is available on all Orato plans. You need an active Google account to connect it.
</Info>

## Connecting Your Gmail Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Gmail settings">
    In your Orato dashboard, go to **CRM Integration → Gmail**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Gmail Account">
    Hit the **Connect Gmail Account** button. A secure authorization window will open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Sign in with the Google account you want notifications sent to, and approve the permissions Orato requests:

    | Permission | Why is it needed |
    | --- | --- |
    | `gmail.send` | To send notification emails to your inbox |
    | `userinfo.email` | To confirm which account is connected |

    That's it, Orato only requests send access. It cannot read your existing emails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Once you approve, your connected email address appears in the status badge at the top of the card. You're all set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your Google account credentials are never stored by Orato. The connection is secured using industry-standard authorization and stored encrypted in your organisation's secure vault.
</Note>

## Choosing Your Notifications

After connecting, you'll see a grid of notification types. Toggle each one on or off depending on what matters to your team.

| Notification | What triggers it |
| --- | --- |
| **Low credits alert** | Credits fall below your defined threshold |
| **System errors** | Critical failures or runtime exceptions in your account |
| **Campaign outcomes** | A campaign finishes, and a summary is ready |
| **Qualified leads** | A lead meets your qualification criteria |
| **Campaign started** | A campaign goes live and begins calling |
| **Campaign ended** | A campaign concludes or is stopped |
| **Appointment booked** | A lead schedules a callback with an advisor |

The counter in the top-right of the section shows how many are currently active (e.g. `5 / 7 active`).

## Setting a Low Credit Threshold

The **Low credits threshold** card lets you define when the credits alert fires.

- Enter a dollar amount in the input field
- Orato will email you when your remaining credits drop to a value below
- The minimum threshold is **\$2.00**

Your connected email address is shown below the input as confirmation of where alerts will land.

---

## Saving Your Preferences

Once you've set your notifications and threshold, click **Save preferences**. Changes take effect immediately.

If you want to undo edits you haven't saved yet, click **Discard changes**. This reloads the last saved state from Orato.

---

## Disconnecting Gmail

To remove the Gmail connection, click **Disconnect Gmail** on the connection card. This immediately stops all email notifications and removes the stored credentials from your vault.

You can reconnect at any time by following the steps above.

---

## Troubleshooting

**I connected, but I'm not receiving emails**

Check that at least one notification type is toggled on, and your preferences are saved. Also, check that Orato emails aren't landing in your spam or promotions folder.

**The connection status isn't updating after I authorized**

Wait a few seconds and refresh the page. Authorization confirmation can occasionally take a moment. If it still shows disconnected, click **Disconnect** and try connecting again.

**I see a "Failed to initialize Gmail connection" error**

The authorization session may have timed out. Click **Connect Gmail Account** again and complete the flow without closing the pop-up window.
