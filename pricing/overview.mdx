## How Orato pricing works

Orato has two distinct cost layers that always stay separate:

**Subscription**: unlocks seats, concurrency, privacy controls, and platform features. Think of it as the access tier. It doesn't ration your usage.

**Credits**: pay-as-you-go and cover actual call activity. They roll forward on every plan, including Free. You only spend credits when calls happen.

That's it. No bundled minutes that vanish at month-end. No surprise overages on a plan you've already paid for.

## Credits: the usage layer

Credits cover what actually happens on a call. They are:

- **Separate from your subscription**: Upgrading your plan doesn't change how credits are priced
- **Pay-as-you-go**: You buy them when needed, in any amount
- **Non-expiring**: They roll forward on every plan, forever, including Free
- **Transparent**: After every call, you can see exactly how many credits were consumed in your call history

<Info>
  For a detailed breakdown of what drives credit consumption per call, see [Call Pricing](/pricing/call-pricing).
</Info>

## Add-ons

**Extra concurrency** is available on any plan, including Free, at **\$5 per slot per month**. Spin up slots before a big campaign and remove them when traffic settles. No plan change required.

Extra seat pricing varies by plan. Pro users get additional seats at a reduced per-seat rate.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a subscription and credits?">
    Your subscription unlocks features and capacity, seats, concurrency, privacy, and analytics. Credits are pay-as-you-go and cover actual call usage. They're billed separately and never expire. You're never paying for minutes you didn't use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do credits expire if I don't use them?">
    No. Credits roll forward forever on every plan, including Free. There are no bundled minutes that vanish at the end of the month; you only spend credits when calls actually happen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much do I save on annual billing?">
    Annual billing is 20% off, that's effectively two months free. Pro drops from $49 to $39/mo, and Pro\+ from $149 to  $119/mo. You're billed once for the year upfront.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=" Can I add more concurrency for a busy campaign?">
    Yes. Extra concurrent call slots are an add-on on any plan, including Free, at \$5 per slot per month. Scale up for a launch and remove slots when traffic settles. No plan change required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans or cancel?">
    Anytime, self-serve. Upgrades apply instantly and prorate; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Cancel whenever you like; your remaining credits stay yours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial for paid plans?">
    You can start on Free at any time with no credit card. When you're ready to move up, Pro and Pro\+ both offer a free trial period so you can test the full feature set before committing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
