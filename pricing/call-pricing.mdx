## How much does Orato cost per call?

Orato uses transparent, usage-based pricing. Every call's total cost is the sum of five components across three parts, Voice AI, telephony, and a platform fee. There are no bundled minutes and no expiry.

---

## What drives a call's cost?

**Part A - Voice AI (STT \+ LLM \+ TTS)**

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Transcriber (STT)" icon="ear-listen">
    Translates the caller's words into text so the AI can comprehend them.

    Billed by **call duration**, rounded to the second.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Language Model (LLM)" icon="brain">
    Reads the transcription, reasons about it, and writes the next response.

    Billed by **tokens**, words, and punctuation pieces read and written.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Voice Synthesis (TTS)" icon="volume-high">
    Converts the AI's text response back into spoken audio that the caller hears.

    Billed by **characters synthesized**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Part B & C — Telephony and Platform**

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Telephony" icon="tower-cell">
    The cost of connecting the call over the phone network. Varies by country and direction (inbound vs. outbound).

    Billed by **call duration**, rounded to the minute.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Orato platform fee" icon="bolt">
    A flat per-minute fee on top of your provider costs. Covers the infrastructure that coordinates everything in real time.

    Billed by **call duration**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  You only pay for what actually happened. A 12-second call is billed for 12 seconds of STT and TTS, not a full minute.
</Info>

## Where to see your call costs

After every call, open **Call History** and click into any conversation. You'll see a full credit breakdown, each component listed separately so you know exactly what drove the cost.

<Tip>
  LLM costs running higher than expected? The first thing to check is your system prompt length. Every word is re-read at the start of every call. A shorter, tighter prompt is the single biggest lever on LLM spend.
</Tip>

## How to reduce your per-call cost

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose lighter models where quality allows">
    A smaller, faster LLM is often sufficient for structured tasks, appointment booking, FAQ handling, and lead qualification. Reserve heavier models for conversations that genuinely need them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your system prompt tight">
    Every word in your prompt adds tokens to every call. Cut anything that doesn't directly shape the assistant's behavior. Use the [Prompting Guide](/getting-started/prompting-guide) to learn what actually needs to be in there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move facts to the Knowledge Base">
    Product details, FAQs, pricing tables, anything static belongs in the Knowledge Base, not the prompt. The AI retrieves it on demand instead of carrying it through every call, which meaningfully reduces token usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before you go live">
    Pro\+ includes free voice and audio testing in a sandbox environment. Catch prompt issues and voice mismatches before spending credits on real calls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credits vs. subscription

Your **plan** (Free, Pro, Pro\+) unlocks features and capacity, seats, concurrency, analytics, and data retention. **Credits** are separate and cover actual call usage.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Subscription" icon="credit-card">
    Unlocks features and raises capacity limits. Does **not** include a credit balance or bundled minutes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Credits" icon="coins">
    Pay-as-you-go. Charged per call based on the five components above. **Never expire** on any plan.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Upgrading your plan does not add credits, and buying credits does not change your plan. They're always separate. See [Pricing Overview](/pricing/overview) for plan details.
</Note>
