## Overview

Phone numbers are what your AI Assistants use to make outbound calls and receive inbound calls. You purchase numbers directly through Orato; no third-party telephony provider setup needed.

## Buying a phone number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Phone Numbers">
    Click **Phone Numbers** in the left sidebar. You'll see all available numbers with their status and country.

    Stats at the top show **the total available** numbers and **Countries** covered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Buy Number">
    Find a number you want and click **Buy Number** next to it. This opens the compliance request form.

    <Info>
      Before any number can be activated, Orato requires a one-time compliance verification to confirm your business identity. This is required by telecom regulations.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your compliance request.">
    <Frame>
      ![Comp](/images/Comp.png)
    </Frame>

    Fill in the compliance form:

    | Field | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Legal Business Name** | Your registered company name, e.g., _Acme Pvt. Ltd._ |
    | **Display Alias** | How you want to appear, e.g., _Acme Support Line_ |
    | **CIN Number** | Company Identification Number |
    | **GST Number** | Your GST registration number |
    | **Country** | Country of business registration |
    | **Number Type** | Type of number being requested |
    | **Registration Certificate** | Upload PDF/PNG/JPEG - max 10MB |
    | **GST Certificate** | Upload PDF/PNG/JPEG - max 10MB |

    Check the confirmation box and click **Submit for Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Orato reviews your documents. Once approved, your number is activated and ready to be assigned to an AI Assistant.

    <Note>
      Compliance is a **one-time process per account**. Once approved, you can purchase additional numbers without resubmitting documents.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compliance tab

Click the **Compliance** tab on the Phone Numbers page to see all your compliance requests:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Request submitted, currently under review |
| **Approved** | Documents verified, numbers are available to purchase |
| **Rejected** | Documents were not accepted. Resubmit with corrections |

<Warning>
  If your compliance request is rejected, you'll receive a reason. Correct the issue in your documents and resubmit via **New Compliance Request**.
</Warning>

---

## Assigning a number to an assistant

Once you have an active phone number, connect it to an AI Assistant:

1. Go to **AI Assistants** and open the assistant you want to use for calls
2. In the **Setup** step, select your phone number in the telephony settings
3. Save the assistant

Your assistant will now use that number for all inbound and outbound calls.
