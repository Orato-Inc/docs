## Overview

Phone numbers are what your AI Assistants use to make outbound calls and receive inbound calls. You purchase numbers directly through Orato, no third-party provider setup needed.

Currently available in:

- 🇺🇸 **United States** (\+1)
- 🇮🇳 **India** (\+91)

---

## Buying a phone number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Phone Numbers">
    Click **Phone Numbers** in the left sidebar. You'll see all available numbers with their status and country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a number">
    Browse the list and find a number that suits you. Click **Buy Number** next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a Compliance Request">
    Before any number can be activated, Orato requires a one-time compliance request to verify your business. You'll be asked to provide:

    | Field | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Legal Business Name** | Your registered company name |
    | **Display Alias** | How you want to appear on caller ID |
    | **CIN Number** | Company Identification Number |
    | **GST Number** | Your GST registration number |
    | **Country** | Country of registration |
    | **Number Type** | Type of number you're requesting |
    | **Registration Certificate** | PDF/PNG/JPEG, max 10MB |
    | **GST Certificate** | PDF/PNG/JPEG, max 10MB |

    Check the confirmation box and click **Submit for Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Orato reviews your compliance documents. Once approved, your number is activated and ready to assign to an AI Assistant.

    <Note>
      Compliance is a one-time process per account. Once approved, you can purchase additional numbers without resubmitting documents.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Compliance tab

The **Compliance** tab shows all your compliance requests and their status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Request submitted, under review |
| **Approved** | Documents verified, numbers can be purchased |
| **Rejected** | Documents were not accepted — resubmit with corrections |

---

## Assigning a number to an assistant

Once you have an active phone number, assign it to an AI Assistant so calls can be made and received:

1. Go to **AI Assistants** and open the assistant you want to assign the number to
2. In the **Setup** step, select your number in the phone/telephony configuration
3. Save the assistant

Your assistant will now use that number for all inbound and outbound calls.
