Your assistants need a phone number to make campaign calls and to receive inbound calls. Numbers are bought from your organization wallet.

<Frame>
  ![Phone numbers page](/images/app/phone-numbers.png)
</Frame>

## Overview stats

| Stat | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Your numbers** | Numbers your organization owns |
| **Avg pickup (30D)** | Average pickup rate over the last 30 days |
| **Best caller ID** | Your best-performing number |
| **Wallet balance** | Funds available. Click **Add funds** to top up. |

## Buy a US number

US numbers are available instantly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Buy a number">
    Go to **Phone Numbers** and click the **Buy a number** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select United States">
    Available numbers are listed with their location and monthly price.

    <Frame>
      ![Available US numbers](/images/app/phone-buy-us.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Buy">
    The monthly price is paid from your wallet and the number appears under **My numbers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy an Indian number

Indian numbers require business verification first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select India">
    On the **Buy a number** tab, select **India**. The **India compliance** form appears.

    <Frame>
      ![India compliance form](/images/app/phone-buy-india.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your company details">
    Enter your **Company legal name**, **GSTIN**, and optionally your **CIN**, then click **Submit for verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    The Orato team reviews requests within 2 to 3 business days. After approval you can browse and buy Indian numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a number

| To... | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Make outbound calls | Set the number as **Outbound caller ID** on an Outbound assistant's [Settings](/assistants/settings) tab |
| Answer inbound calls | Set it as **Inbound phone number** on an Inbound assistant's Settings tab |

<Note>
  You can test any assistant in the browser without a number.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Calling Indian leads? Use an Indian number as caller ID. People are far more likely to answer a local number.
</Tip>
