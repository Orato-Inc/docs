The **Meetings** page (Meeting Scheduler) keeps track of meetings with your leads, such as site visits, demos, and consultations.

<Frame>
  ![Meeting Scheduler](/images/app/meetings.png)
</Frame>

## Connect Google Calendar

Connecting your calendar lets Orato:

- Sync meetings both ways
- Check availability in real time to prevent conflicts
- Show all meetings in one dashboard
- Send calendar invitations to clients
- Send notifications and reminders

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meetings">
    Click **Meetings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect Google Calendar** and sign in with the Google account you use for meetings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can skip this and connect later from settings or the [Integrations](/integrations/google-calendar) page.

## Create a meeting manually

Click **Create meeting manually** (or the create button once you have meetings).

<Frame>
  ![Schedule New Meeting dialog](/images/app/meetings-create.png)
</Frame>

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select Lead** | Yes | The lead you're meeting |
| **Meeting Title** | Yes | For example `Property Viewing - Downtown Apartment` |
| **Description** | No | Agenda or notes |
| **Date & Time** | Yes | |
| **Duration (minutes)** | Yes | Defaults to 60 |
| **Location** | No | Defaults to "To be confirmed" |
| **Meeting Type** | Yes | For example **Consultation** |

Click **Schedule Meeting** to save.

<Tip>
  Today's meetings also appear in the **Today's schedule** panel on the [Dashboard](/dashboard).
</Tip>

## Book meetings from calls

Tell your assistant when to propose a meeting in its instructions, for example:

```text
If the lead is qualified, offer a site visit this week and confirm a day and time that suits them.
```

Then review qualified calls on the [Calls](/calls) page and schedule the meeting here, or let your team follow up.
