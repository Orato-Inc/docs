## What is the Agents' Library?

The Agents Library is a collection of ready-made assistant configurations built around the most common calling use cases. Each template comes with pre-written instructions, a suggested voice, a call flow, and sensible defaults for things like silence timeout and max duration.

You're not locked into any of it. Every template is a starting point; you open it, adjust the instructions for your business, and save.

## How to use a template

1. Go to **AI Assistants** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Create Assistant**.
3. On the goal-selection screen, you'll see the four built-in goal presets. These map to the most common templates.
4. Pick the one closest to your use case.
5. On the next screen, fill in your company name, business description, and call goal, or use the **AI instruction generator** to draft the system message automatically.
6. Review the generated instructions, edit as needed, and continue through the setup wizard.

The templates pre-populate the system message, opening line, and closing message based on the goal you picked. You're reviewing and refining, not starting from scratch.

## Available templates

### Sales & Lead Outreach

**Best for:** outbound campaigns, lead qualification, demo booking, cold outreach

The sales template sets the assistant up as an outbound caller that introduces your product, qualifies the lead with a few questions, and tries to book a next step — a demo, a callback with a human, or a site visit. The default call flow covers: greeting by name, checking if they have a moment, giving a 2-sentence pitch, qualifying, and closing with an action.

Good starting point for: real estate follow-ups, financial product outreach, SaaS demo booking, insurance qualification, solar or home services leads.

---

### Customer Support

**Best for:** inbound calls, issue resolution, escalation routing

The support template sets the assistant up to answer your phone number, identify what the caller needs, resolve common questions, and escalate to a human when it can't help. The default instructions include: warm greeting, issue identification, resolution attempt, and a clean handoff phrase if escalation is needed.

Good starting point for: clinics managing appointment calls, e-commerce handling order status, SaaS handling basic account questions, any business that gets repetitive inbound calls.

---

### Website Voice Chat

**Best for:** web widget, site visitors, lead capture

The widget template is designed for the web chat assistant that embeds on your website. It greets visitors, answers questions about your product or service, and nudges interested visitors toward a next step, booking a demo, filling out a form, or connecting with your team. It's built for shorter, more exploratory conversations than a phone call.

Good starting point for: SaaS product pages, service business websites, real estate portals, any site where visitors have questions before converting.

---

### Custom

**Best for:** anything that doesn't fit the above

Starts with a blank system message and outbound defaults. Use this if you have a very specific use case, a non-standard call flow, or if you're importing instructions you've already written.

This is also the right choice for API-triggered calls, where your backend passes in dynamic variables and the call logic is more complex.

## Customizing a template

Every template is editable in full. The parts you'll most commonly want to change:

**Identity**: Replace the placeholder company name with yours. Add a sentence or two about what your company actually does.

**Call flow**: The numbered steps in the instructions are a guide, not a script. Reorder them to match your actual sales or support process. Add steps for objections you commonly hear.

**Qualifying questions**: Templates use generic questions. Replace them with the specific things your team asks to qualify a lead.

**Escalation**: If you have a transfer number set up, add it in the **Transfer to Human** field in the advanced settings. The assistant will bridge the call to that number when someone asks for a human.

**Voice**: The Personality step of the wizard lets you pick a voice from the library and preview it before saving. Try a few before settling on one — the voice has a big impact on how the assistant comes across.

## After deploying a template

Run a few test calls using the **Talk** button on the assistant before going live. Go through the flow as a skeptical caller, push back on the pitch, ask something unexpected, say you're busy. This surfaces gaps in the instructions before real leads hear them.

After your first batch of real calls, read through a handful of transcripts on the **Transcript & Search** page. Look for moments where the assistant gave a confusing answer or lost the thread. Update the instructions to handle those cases, and you'll see the quality of outcomes improve over the next campaign.
