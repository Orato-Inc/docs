By the end of this guide you'll have a working AI assistant you can talk to in your browser, plus a clear path to your first real phone call.

<Info>
  You need an Orato account. Sign up or log in at [app.tryorato.com](https://app.tryorato.com).
</Info>

## 1. Create an assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Assistants">
    In the sidebar, click **AI Assistants**. Start from one of the **Agent templates** or click **+ Create Assistant** to start blank.

    <Frame>
      ![AI Assistants page with agent templates](/images/app/assistants.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the prompt">
    On the **Prompt** tab:

    - Enter an **Agent name**, for example `Priya`.
    - Optionally add a **First message**, for example `Hi {{customer_name}}, this is Priya calling from Acme Realty.`
    - Under **Generate & refine**, pick a conversation style (**Follow my steps** or **Chat naturally**), describe your agent in a sentence or two, and click **Generate**. Orato writes the instructions for you.

    <Frame>
      ![Prompt tab](/images/app/assistant-prompt.png)
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Have a website? Click **Import from website**. Orato imports your pages into the knowledge base and helps you confirm business facts for the prompt.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a voice">
    On the **Voice** tab, pick a provider (Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Sarvam, or Smallest AI), a voice, and the speed. Click **Preview** to hear it. Set the **Primary language** the agent starts in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role and AI model">
    On the **Settings** tab, choose the **Role**:

    - **Outbound** to call leads
    - **Inbound** to answer calls to your number
    - **Web Widget** to talk to visitors on your website

    Then choose the **AI Brain** provider and model. The default, Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5, is a good starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Assistant**. You can come back and edit any tab later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Test it in your browser

You don't need a phone number to test. Open your assistant and use **Talk** to start a live voice conversation in the browser. Try the questions your customers usually ask and listen for anything that sounds off.

After each test, open **Conversations** to review what happened and refine the prompt.

## 3. Go live

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Outbound calls">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Buy a number">
        Go to **Phone Numbers → Buy a number** and buy a US number, or submit India compliance for an Indian number. See [Phone numbers](/phone-numbers).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the caller ID">
        In your assistant's **Settings** tab, choose the number under **Outbound caller ID**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add leads">
        Go to **Leads → Create batch**. Add contacts manually or upload a CSV/Excel file with `name` and `phone` columns.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Call">
        For one lead, go to **Calls → Make call**. For a whole batch, go to **Campaigns → Create campaign**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Inbound calls">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Buy a number">
        Buy a number on the **Phone Numbers** page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect it to the assistant">
        Set the assistant's role to **Inbound** and choose the number under **Inbound phone number** on the **Settings** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Call it">
        Call the number from your phone. Your assistant answers.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Website widget">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Style the widget">
        Set the assistant's role to **Web Widget** and customize colors and copy on the **Branding** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a public key">
        Go to **Developer → API Keys → Create API Key**, choose **Public Key**, and add your website domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install the script">
        Paste the snippet into your site:

        ```html
        <script
          src="https://app.tryorato.com/widget.js"
          data-key="pk_live_YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY"
          data-assistant="YOUR_ASSISTANT_ID"
          defer
        ></script>
        ```
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Before you go live

<Check>Test the assistant with **Talk** and review the transcript in **Conversations**.</Check>
<Check>Attach knowledge documents if the assistant needs to answer product or pricing questions.</Check>
<Check>Add a **Transfer to Human** number in **Advanced** so callers can reach a person.</Check>
<Check>Top up your wallet in **Settings → Billing & Credits**.</Check>
