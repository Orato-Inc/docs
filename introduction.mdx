## What is Orato?

Orato is a voice AI platform for businesses that sell and support customers over the phone. You build **AI Assistants**: voice agents that hold natural, real-time conversations on your behalf. Each assistant can call your leads, answer your business number, or chat with visitors on your website.

Around the assistants, Orato gives you a lightweight CRM: lead batches, outbound campaigns, call logs, conversation summaries, meetings, and a live dashboard.

## How a conversation works

Every conversation, on a phone call or in the website widget, runs through a real-time pipeline:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transcriber turns speech into text">
    The caller speaks and a speech-to-text model (Deepgram or Sarvam AI) transcribes it in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The AI model decides what to say">
    A large language model reads your instructions and the conversation so far, then writes the reply. Choose from Anthropic, Groq, OpenAI, Sarvam AI, or Google models.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice engine speaks the reply">
    A text-to-speech engine (Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Sarvam, or Smallest AI) turns the reply into natural speech the caller hears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Read [Platform concepts](/platform) for a deeper look at each stage.

## Platform overview

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Live overview of leads, calls, outcomes, assistant performance, and spend |
| **Leads** | Organize contacts into batches you can target with campaigns |
| **Calls** | Monitor every call with status, direction, duration, and outcome |
| **Conversations** | Review voice and chat interactions with summaries and billing details |
| **Phone Numbers** | Buy US and Indian numbers for caller ID and inbound calls |
| **Campaigns** | Run outbound calling across a lead batch |
| **Integrations** | Connect ticketing, calendar, messaging, and notification tools |
| **Meetings** | Schedule and track client meetings |
| **AI Assistants** | Create and configure your voice agents |
| **Knowledge Base** | Upload documents or import websites for your assistants to reference |
| **Developer** | Create API keys and install the website widget |
