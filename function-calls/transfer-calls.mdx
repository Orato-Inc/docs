## Overview

There are situations where a human needs to take over, a caller is upset, the question is too complex, or the caller specifically asks for a person. Call transfer lets your assistant hand off smoothly instead of failing awkwardly.

---

## Transfer types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Warm Transfer">
    Your assistant stays on the call briefly and introduces the caller to the human agent before dropping off.

    **What it sounds like:** _"I'm going to connect you with one of our team members now. Please hold for just a moment."_

    The human agent joins the call, the assistant provides a brief context handoff, and then drops.

    **Best for:** Customer support, complex sales calls, situations where context matters.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cold Transfer">
    Your assistant transfers the call directly to the human agent without any introduction. The caller is simply routed to a new number.

    **What it sounds like:** _"Let me transfer you now."_, and the call is routed immediately.

    **Best for:** Simple routing, when the receiving agent already has the caller's context.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Setting up call transfer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to AI Assistants and open your assistant">
    Click **AI Assistants** in the sidebar and open the assistant you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Tools Tab">
    In the assistant's Setup, navigate to the **Tools Tab**. This is where you add callable actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Transfer tool">
    Add a new tool and configure:

    | Field | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Tool name** | e.g. `transfer_to_human` |
    | **Transfer number** | The phone number to transfer calls to |
    | **Transfer type** | Warm or Cold |
    | **Description** | Tell the assistant when to use this tool — e.g. _"Use this when the caller asks to speak to a human or when you cannot answer their question"_ |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your instructions">
    Add a line to your assistant's instructions that tells it when to trigger the transfer:

    ```text
        If the caller explicitly asks to speak to a human, 
            or if you've been unable to help after two attempts, 
                use the transfer tool immediately.
                    Do not keep trying — transfer straight away.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Use the **Talk** button to test your assistant. Say something like _"I want to speak to a real person"_ and verify the transfer triggers correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Writing good transfer instructions

<Tip>
  Be explicit about when to transfer. _"Transfer if the caller seems frustrated"_ is too vague. _"Transfer if the caller says the words 'speak to a human', 'real person', or 'manager'"_ is clear and reliable.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Add a brief transition phrase to your instructions so the transfer sounds smooth — e.g. _"Before transferring, say: 'I'll connect you with one of our team members right away.'"_
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Make sure the transfer number is always available and staffed during calling hours. A failed transfer is worse than the AI handling it — the caller hears a dead line.
</Warning>
