## The voice pipeline

Every conversation on Orato flows through three stages that repeat for each turn: the transcriber listens, the AI model thinks, and the voice engine speaks.

<Frame>
  ![Orato voice pipeline: transcriber, AI model, voice engine](/images/voice-pipeline.svg)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transcriber: your assistant's ears">
    Converts the caller's speech into text in real time. Orato picks a transcriber automatically based on the languages you choose. You can override it in [Advanced settings](/assistants/advanced#transcriber) with **Deepgram** (Flux English, Flux Multilingual) or **Sarvam AI** (Saaras v4 Realtime, Saaras v3).
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI model: your assistant's brain">
    A large language model reads your instructions, the knowledge you attached, and the conversation history, then writes the next reply. Providers: **Anthropic**, **Groq**, **OpenAI**, **Sarvam AI**, and **Google**. See [Providers](/providers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice engine: your assistant's voice">
    Converts the reply into speech. Providers: **Cartesia**, **ElevenLabs**, **Sarvam**, and **Smallest AI**, each with its own voices and speed range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AI Assistant" icon="robot">
    A configured voice agent. It has a name, a first message, instructions, a voice, an AI model, and call settings. One assistant can handle many conversations. See [AI Assistants](/ai-assistant).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Role" icon="user-gear">
    Every assistant has one role that decides how it is deployed:

    | Role | What it does |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Outbound** (Call my leads) | Dials your contact list and qualifies leads. Used by campaigns and the **Make call** button. |
    | **Inbound** (Answer incoming calls) | Picks up calls to one of your phone numbers automatically. |
    | **Web Widget** (Embed on my website, beta) | Lets visitors start a voice call or a text chat from your site. |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Instructions (prompt)" icon="file-lines">
    The written guide your assistant follows on every conversation: who it is, what the goal is, how to speak, and what to avoid. Orato can generate instructions for you and structure them into sections. See [Prompt](/assistants/prompt).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Variables" icon="brackets-curly">
    Placeholders such as `{{customer_name}}` that Orato fills in for each lead, so the assistant can greet people by name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lead batch" icon="users">
    A named group of contacts, created manually or from a CSV or Excel file. Campaigns call one batch at a time. See [Leads](/leads).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Campaign" icon="bullhorn">
    Connects a lead batch to an outbound assistant and calls every lead at the call rate you set, immediately or on a schedule. See [Campaigns](/campaigns).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Conversation" icon="messages">
    A record of one voice or chat interaction, with contact, assistant, duration, summary, outcome, and billing. See [Conversations](/conversations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Knowledge base" icon="book">
    Documents (PDF, TXT, DOCX) and imported website pages your assistant searches during calls and widget chats. See [Knowledge base](/knowledge-base).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wallet" icon="wallet">
    Your prepaid USD balance. Calls and phone numbers are paid from the wallet. See [Billing and credits](/account/billing).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Cost per minute

While you build an assistant, Orato shows a live per-minute estimate at the top of the editor, in USD and INR, broken down by component:

| Component | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Transcriber** | Speech-to-text |
| **LLM** | The AI model's processing, which varies by provider and model |
| **Voice** | Text-to-speech, which varies by voice provider |
| **Platform** | Orato platform fee |

The estimate updates as you change providers, so you can compare options before saving. For example, a default setup with Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5 and a Cartesia voice shows about **$0.059 per minute**. Phone number rental is billed separately (see [Phone numbers](/phone-numbers)).

<Note>
  Figures in this documentation reflect the dashboard at the time of writing. Always check the live estimate in the assistant editor for current pricing.
</Note>
