## Overview

Instead of writing instructions from scratch, start from a template. Each template is built for a specific use case, with a ready-to-use prompt, a recommended LLM and voice, and a clear goal already defined. You just fill in your company name and adjust the details.

## Available templates

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lead Qualifier" icon="filter">
    Calls inbound or outbound leads, asks discovery questions, scores them, and routes hot leads to your sales team.

    **Best for:** Sales teams, real estate, SaaS, financial services
  </Card>

  <Card title="Appointment Booker" icon="calendar-check">
    Qualifies the caller and books a meeting directly during the call, synced to your connected calendar.

    **Best for:** Consultants, clinics, service businesses, property agents

  <Card title="Customer Support" icon="headset">
    Answers FAQs, handles common issues, and transfers to a human when the caller needs real help.

  </Card>

  <Card title="Payment Reminder" icon="credit-card">

## How to use a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to AI Assistants">
    Click **AI Assistants** in the left sidebar, then click **\+ Create Assistant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    On the creation screen, browse the available templates and pick the one closest to your use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise the instructions">
    The template comes with a ready-to-use prompt. Update:

    - Your assistant's name
    - Your company name and what you do
    - Specific qualifying criteria or questions for your business
    - Any objections or scenarios unique to your industry
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your LLM and voice">
    Each template includes a recommended provider. You can change it, but the recommendation is a good starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and launch">
    Click **Talk** to test the assistant live in your browser. Adjust the instructions until it sounds right, then launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Survey Collector | Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) | Cartesia sonic-2 | 3–5 min |
