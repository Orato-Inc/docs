## Use a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Assistants">
    Click **AI Assistants** in the sidebar. Templates are listed under **Agent templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a template">
    The editor opens with the template's first message and instructions filled in.

    <Frame>
      ![Real Estate Lead Qualifier template loaded in the editor](/images/app/assistant-template.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it yours">
    - Enter an **Agent name**.
    - Replace generic details (company name, city, products, prices) with your own.
    - Use **Generate & refine** to add follow-ups, such as `Also ask whether they need a home loan`.
    - Attach your own documents under **Advanced → Knowledge documents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose voice and settings">
    Pick a voice on the **Voice** tab and a role and AI model on the **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and test">
    Click **Create Assistant**, then test with **Talk** before going live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's inside a template

The Real Estate Lead Qualifier shows the typical structure:

- **Identity**: who the agent is ("Priya, a professional real estate calling assistant").
- **Goal**: what a successful call looks like (book a site visit or a callback).
- **Personality**: warm, respectful, short replies, never pushy.
- **Qualification flow**: the questions to cover, in a natural order.
- **Rules**: what never to do, such as inventing project names or quoting exact prices.

Use the same structure when you write your own prompts. See the [Prompting guide](/prompting-guide).

<Tip>
  Templates use placeholders such as `{customer_name}` and `{date}`. Keep them so Orato can fill in each lead's details.
</Tip>
